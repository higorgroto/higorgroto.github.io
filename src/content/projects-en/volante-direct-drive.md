## Objective

Develop a high-performance, low-cost Direct Drive steering wheel for racing simulators, using a hoverboard motor for Force Feedback.

## Technical Specifications

- **Motor:** Hoverboard BLDC (36V, 350W)
- **Driver:** MKS XDrive Mini
- **Communication:** USB Type-C
- **Encoder:** MT6701 magnetic encoder
- **Maximum torque:** 10 Nm
- **Frame:** 3D printing in PETG
- **Power Supply:** Switching power supply (24V, 20A)

## System Architecture

The electronics are based on FFBeast (https://ffbeast.github.io/docs/en/wheel.html), an open-source project. The differentiator was the frame we built. Fusion 360 was used for the 3D modeling, including the parts used. The components were purchased on AliExpress and OLX.
